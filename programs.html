The Jones Center, Springdale

Summer of Support Project

Our first official event as a nonprofit, held just before back-to-school. Students received backpacks filled with school supplies and essential items, and families enjoyed:

  • Free meals provided by Tyson Foods
  • Bounce houses and a snow cone stand
  • Family-friendly activities
  • Community resource vendors
600meals served
200backpacks given out

With support from Tyson Foods, Sam’s Club, Office Depot, and other partners.

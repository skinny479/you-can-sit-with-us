The You Can Sit With Us Foundation started as a simple message of inclusion. Today we show up for Northwest Arkansas kids with school supplies, free meals, and a clear message: you belong here.

Our symbol

There’s always room on the bench.

A bench is built to be shared. Scoot over, and there’s room for one more. That’s the whole idea behind You Can Sit With Us: no one should have to sit alone, at lunch, on the playground, or anywhere else.

What we do

Small acts that say “you belong.”

Back-to-school backpacks

We send students into the new school year with backpacks filled with school supplies and essential items.

Free family meals

Our events serve free hot meals so families can gather, eat, and connect.

A message of inclusion

Everything we do comes back to one idea: every kid deserves a place where they're welcome.

Signature event

The Summer of Support Project

Our Summer of Support event filled the Jones Center in Springdale with backpacks of school supplies, free meals, bounce houses, snow cones, and community resource vendors for local families.
